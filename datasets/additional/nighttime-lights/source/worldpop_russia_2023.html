Nighttime Lights 2015-2023 (flares/volcanoes filtered), Russia

The dataset is available to download in Geotiff format at a resolution of 3 arc seconds (approximately 100m at the equator). The projection is Geographic Coordinate System, WGS84 (EPSG:4326).
The value of each grid cell represents annual average radiance (in nW/cm2/sr), with Flares and Volcanoes filtered out.

Data Source: This product was made utilizing Annual average-masked VNL 2.1/2.2 data produced by the Earth Observation Group, Payne Institute for Public Policy, Colorado School of Mines: Elvidge, C.D.; Zhizhin, M.; Ghosh, T.; Hsu, F.-C.; Taneja, J. Annual Time Series of Global VIIRS Nighttime Lights Derived from Monthly Averages: 2012 to 2019. Remote Sens. 2021, 13, 922. https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13050922

Region : Russian Federation
DOI : 10.5258/SOTON/WP00772
Date of production : 2024-01-01
